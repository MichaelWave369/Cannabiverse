What this test establishes, and what it cannot

A correctly matched file hash connects one test to known real photo bytes. Segmentation on those bytes is an actual real-image experiment, but the marked pixels are self-asserted labels, not an independent annotated dataset. To claim reliable whole-image performance we still need a complete, independently checked leaf mask for every test image, multiple physical plants, and a frozen, genuinely untouched holdout. Even perfect leaf-color segmentation does not establish vascular connectivity or real network transport.
