LeafLab Research Audit

An intentionally skeptical laboratory. Assemble annotated leaf-tip observations, hold out entire plant IDs, compare simple mathematical models, and report when golden-ratio predictions lose. Study files and reports stay in your browser until you export them.

No discovery claims. Manual source data and plant IDs are unverified. A small held-out model comparison is not proof of predictive generality. The golden-ratio shape is an explicit test candidate, not a known cannabis law. The separate 3/9 count audit does not test 6 and cannot establish biological numerology.

3 · 3/9 count pattern versus controls

A specific operational definition of the old 3-6-9 idea: the fraction of plants whose consistently observed leaflet count is 3 or 9, compared with non-cannabis palmate controls. Each plant counts once; plants containing multiple observed count classes are excluded from this audit. Because our current complete-tip workflow supports odd counts only, 6 is not evaluated.

Add both cannabis and control observations for a comparison.

The conditional label-permutation reference only describes the recorded sample, under an assumption that group labels would be exchangeable. It does not correct for growth stage, cultivar, taxon selection, observer choice or multiple exploration. A significant value would not demonstrate a biological 3-6-9 law.
