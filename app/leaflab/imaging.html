Φ LeafLab Imaging Lab

Measure what an image algorithm actually sees. Select a leaf-colored pixel and a background pixel, review an experimental connected-region mask, and inspect dark vein-like ridge candidates. Compare the result with a manual leaflet-outline record before treating it as useful data.

These are color and contrast algorithms, not a trained leaf recognizer. A dark line could be a vein, shadow, fold, serration or camera artifact. The extracted graph measures pixels, not real xylem or vascular flow. No remote image upload or botanical discovery claims.
