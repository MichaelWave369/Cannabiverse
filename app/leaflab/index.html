Φ Leaf Morphology Lab

Explore synthetic cannabis leaf architecture, fit manually marked leaflet tips, calibrate photos against a ruler, trace individual leaflet outlines and compare saved specimens. All measurements stay inside your browser. Open the v0.4 independent-plant research audit for held-out geometry and 3/9-count comparisons, or inspect a local image in Imaging Lab v0.6.

Model ≠ measurement ≠ biological mechanism. Generated leaves are hypothetical silhouettes in arbitrary units. Venation is schematic, not a flow simulation. Neither Fibonacci patterns nor 3-6-9 structure nor an efficient natural design has been established by these drawings.

Geometry controls

This initial model has no genotype, developmental, light-interception, pressure-flow, or structural-strength parameters.

Click the leaflet junction first, then each visible leaflet tip. Photos are never uploaded; the browser uses a temporary local object URL.

Research boundaries

Photo mode measures hub-to-tip directions and relative lengths only. The v0.2 tip fit is descriptive and in-sample. v0.3 manual scale and polygon metrics estimate image-plane dimensions only; group comparisons are descriptive, not inferential. It does not validate predictions on held-out leaves or identify serrations, photosynthesis, vein topology, or real-world units. Perspective and scale must be independently controlled before comparing specimens.
