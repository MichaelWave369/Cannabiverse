Φ Leaf Morphology Lab

Explore the architecture of a palmately compound cannabis leaf: leaflet fan geometry, serrations, apparent symmetry, and schematic vein branching. Compare generated geometries or annotate your own photograph, entirely in your browser.

Model ≠ measurement ≠ biological mechanism. Generated leaves are hypothetical silhouettes in arbitrary units. Venation is schematic, not a flow simulation. Neither Fibonacci patterns nor 3-6-9 structure nor an efficient natural design has been established by these drawings.

Geometry controls

This initial model has no genotype, developmental, light-interception, pressure-flow, or structural-strength parameters.

Research boundaries

Photo mode measures hub-to-tip directions and relative lengths only. It does not derive serrations, photosynthesis, vein topology, or real-world units. Perspective and scale must be independently controlled before comparing specimens.
