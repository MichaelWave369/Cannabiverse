Φ Network Engineering Lab

Test whether a routing strategy survives repeated, reproducible synthetic workloads. Allocate an equal number of link-capacity slots across network topologies, then compare throughput, queue losses, latency and the geometry-weighted cost of each capacity allocation.

Network engineering sandbox, not botanical evidence. All links, queues and packets are synthetic. Pixel-based wire cost is an abstract proxy, not dollars, measured bandwidth or real vascular transport. Equal total capacity slots do not guarantee equal cost or latency. No result establishes natural network superiority.

Engineering boundaries

Traffic is identical for every network shape and algorithm within one seed. Repeating seeds measures sensitivity to traffic selections, not uncertainty about real plant physiology. Short-link-first costs less in this proxy but may concentrate resources in the wrong places for network demand.
