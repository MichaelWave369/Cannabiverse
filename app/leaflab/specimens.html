Φ Specimen Provenance Lab

Collect a source-photo manifest, verify its SHA-256 against original local image bytes, link separately annotated v0.7 references, and report which study-quality fields remain unverified or missing. No image is uploaded or saved by this tool.

Byte identity ≠ botanical authentication. A matching SHA-256 proves only that selected local bytes match their recorded hash. It cannot identify a plant, detect an edited photo, confirm legal permission, authenticate an expert or demonstrate blinded observers. This lab makes missing evidence visible rather than manufacturing certainty.

No audit. A complete checklist is not a claim of anatomical correctness, taxonomic confirmation or permission verification.

What this version intentionally does not do

It does not send photos to a server, embed image files in JSON, infer anatomy, classify species, check legal authorization, or automatically assert that an observer was blind. Protocol metadata is user-entered and can be mistaken or falsified. No real plant specimen dataset or authenticated expert review is included with Cannabiverse.
