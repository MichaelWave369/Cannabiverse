Φ Evidence & Validation Gateway

Pair independently drawn line references on the same original image, audit human-observer disagreement, and explicitly assign whole plants to development or holdout sets before summarizing detector scores. Imported reports remain user-asserted data, never automatically promoted to verified botanical truth.

Source integrity, not source authentication: JSON SHA-256 values and plant/observer IDs are self-reported. This page never opens the original photographs or checks the SHA-256 against their bytes. Agreement between drawings is not proof of real vascular anatomy. “Holdout” is a user-declared role and does not prove an untouched experiment.
