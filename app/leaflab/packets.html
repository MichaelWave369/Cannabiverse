Φ BioNetwork Simulator

Route synthetic packets through a manually marked image-line graph and compare three routing heuristics over annotated, minimum-spanning-tree, and star-network shapes. Apply congestion and failures while keeping offered traffic, node positions and link capacities the same.

Simulation ≠ nature. These links are arbitrary network channels, not validated leaf veins or observed vascular transport. One simulation tick is an abstract time step, one packet is an artificial traffic unit, and equal per-edge capacity is a modeling assumption. Results cannot demonstrate that a cannabis leaf is a superior router.

Three competing strategies

Shortest distance: minimum 2D sum of link lengths. Queue-aware: penalizes occupied links and downstream queues. Bridge-avoiding: also penalizes links with no alternate route, if an alternative path exists. Neither adaptive heuristic is guaranteed to win.
