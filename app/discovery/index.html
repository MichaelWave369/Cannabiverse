Φ Cannabiverse · Discovery Lab v0.1 · Research screening only

Find patterns. Not imaginary molecules.

A local-first exploratory screen for high-resolution mass-feature tables. Enter feature data, compare CH₂-separated neutral masses, and inspect quality-control flags. Your table stays in this browser; there is no upload, remote database request containing it, or account requirement.

Not a cannabinoid identification system. A Kendrick mass relationship can arise from many chemical families, contaminants, and artifacts. This release does not analyze raw instrument spectra, perform MassQL searches, validate fragment ions, establish natural occurrence, or generate medical claims.
