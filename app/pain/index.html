Pain & the Endocannabinoid System

Carefully separated evidence from a 2025 AHRQ systematic review, receptor biology, NIH safety guidance, and a professional society position. These are sourced research summaries, not a personal treatment protocol.

Human evidence boundary: Studied THC-containing products can have small short-term benefits for certain chronic pain patients, while oral CBD alone did not show pain improvement in the reviewed trials. Dizziness, sedation and nausea matter. Whole-plant and long-term outcomes remain uncertain.
