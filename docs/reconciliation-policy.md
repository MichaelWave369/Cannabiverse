# Reconciliation Policy

Cannabiverse does not maintain one mutable list called “all cannabinoids.”

Instead it preserves historical inventories and appends reconciliation events.

## Baseline

The 2021 Radwan review's 125-record catalogue is retained as an immutable historical baseline.

That does **not** mean 125 is the current global number of known phytocannabinoids.

## Reconciliation

Later literature may:
- add a new canonical structure,
- confirm natural occurrence of a previously known structure,
- downgrade an occurrence claim,
- identify an artifact,
- resolve a synonym,
- add a relationship,
- or contradict an earlier classification.

Those changes are represented as dated receipts.

## Naming conflicts

When source nomenclature is inconsistent, Cannabiverse preserves the source wording and records the conflict. It does not silently invent a corrected trivial name.

## Counting

Release counts describe the contents of that Cannabiverse release, not a universal final total for Cannabis chemistry.
