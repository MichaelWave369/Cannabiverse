# Evidence Model

Cannabiverse stores evidence on independent axes rather than averaging unlike facts.

## Why no universal confidence score?

A compound may have:

- strong structural identity;
- weak evidence that it naturally occurs in Cannabis;
- unknown biosynthetic origin;
- interesting in-vitro activity;
- no reliable human evidence;
- incomplete safety data.

A single number would hide those differences.

## Current lanes

- identity: I0–I4
- occurrence: O0–O4
- biosynthesis: B0–B3

Future source/claim objects may add independent lanes for assay quality, replication, biological activity, human evidence, and safety.

## Evidence ceiling

Evidence cannot make a stronger claim than the experiment supports. Examples:

- MS fragmentation alone does not equal isolated structural confirmation.
- receptor binding does not equal human potency.
- user experience does not generalize to a population.
- a biosynthetic hypothesis does not become a pathway because a graph renderer draws an arrow.
