Designed for disagreement

Historical records remain reproducible. New evidence appends receipts instead of silently rewriting the past. Provisional detections stay provisional. Hypotheses stay hypotheses. Artifact cautions travel with the occurrence claim.

Scope

Cannabiverse is research and educational infrastructure. It is not medical advice, a dosing system, or a mechanism for turning chemical detection into clinical claims. The current 145-record canon is a versioned reconciliation set, not a claim that Cannabis contains exactly 145 phytocannabinoids.

Cannabiverse · MIT-licensed code · Scientific sources retain their original licenses and citation requirements.
