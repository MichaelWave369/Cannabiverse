# Cannabiverse Governance

## Prime rule

**IDENTITY ≠ OCCURRENCE ≠ BIOSYNTHESIS ≠ BIOACTIVITY ≠ HUMAN EFFECT ≠ SAFETY**

No model, interface, query, contributor, or graph operation may collapse these lanes into a single implied truth level.

## Authority rules

1. **Capability does not grant authority.** Search, inference, visualization, or model output cannot promote evidence.
2. **Nodes require provenance.** Confirmed canonical records must resolve to a registered source.
3. **Edges require provenance.** A chemically plausible relationship is not an established edge unless evidence and source are encoded.
4. **Hypotheses stay hypotheses.** They must never render or export as established biosynthesis.
5. **Historical baselines are immutable.** Corrections append reconciliation receipts.
6. **Provisional is not confirmed.** Putative detections and search targets remain outside confirmed canon.
7. **Artifacts are first-class.** Isolation, storage, thermal, oxidative, and analytical artifacts must be representable without being mistaken for plant biosynthesis.
8. **Absence is scoped.** "Not detected" means not detected in the cited experiment.
9. **Aliases do not merge structures automatically.** Stereochemistry, isomerism, molecular form, and source context are preserved.
10. **Applications cannot override the ledger.**

## Evidence lanes

### Identity
- **I0** unknown / no reliable identification
- **I1** unresolved analytical signal
- **I2** putative identity or historical review catalogue
- **I3** validated targeted identity and/or reference-standard match
- **I4** isolated structural characterization and/or unequivocal standard confirmation

### Occurrence
- **O0** no occurrence evidence / not detected in cited source
- **O1** historical review-catalogued occurrence
- **O2** putative detection in Cannabis
- **O3** confirmed analytical occurrence
- **O4** isolated from Cannabis/hemp and structurally confirmed

### Biosynthesis
- **B0** unknown / not assessed
- **B1** plausible or source-proposed route
- **B2** correlative / targeted metabolomic support
- **B3** established pathway or enzyme evidence

## Promotion rule

A record moves to a stronger state only when a new receipt supports that exact lane. Stronger identity does not automatically strengthen occurrence. Receptor binding does not establish human potency. Correlation does not establish enzyme causation.

## Change discipline

Every scientific change should be reviewable as:

```text
previous state
+ new source receipt
+ explicit reconciliation action
= new state
```

Never silently mutate an old claim because newer literature exists.

## What CI means

A green validation run means the repository satisfies structural governance contracts. It does **not** mean every scientific claim has been independently replicated.


## Correspondence rule

**CORRESPONDENCE ≠ MECHANISM**

A governed structural correspondence may identify an element- and connectivity-preserving mapping between checked-in molecular graphs. That does not establish a reaction mechanism, transition state, kinetic pathway, hydrogen-transfer path, or physical conformational trajectory.

Transformation visualizations must consume explicit correspondence receipts. They may not promote a visual resemblance or animation into mechanistic authority.


## Bond-delta rule

**RAW SDF BOND-ORDER DELTA ≠ CHEMICAL BOND CHANGE ≠ MECHANISM**

A source/target SDF pair may encode the same mapped connectivity with different single/double bond-order assignments. Such differences are first-class representation receipts, not automatically chemical transformation claims.

A bond-order difference may be promoted only by a future normalization or evidence process that explicitly earns that authority. Visualization must preserve the distinction.
